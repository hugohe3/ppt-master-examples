The takeaway is that Paper2Reel is a presentation surface rather than a fourth generated summary: one alignment sidecar makes a poster block, a video segment, a slide thumbnail, and a blog passage into different views of the same section. Figure 9 shows the two interaction states from Section 2.5.1: hovering a poster section highlights it while neighbors fade, and double-clicking opens the section modal with video and captions on the left, a language switch in the header, and the aligned blog passage on the right. The viewer is poster-first because the poster is the most compact map of the paper; playback uses the subtitle-free source with captions from sidecars so the toggle never doubles subtitles. The alignment sidecar maps each canonical section id to its poster block, slide targets, video window, subtitle tracks, thumbnails, and blog blocks; the viewer never scrapes filenames or the video. Invoked on a PDF, an arXiv link, or a partial bundle, it completes missing stages through the full upstream workflows so section ids and paths stay consistent. The gates on the right validate the reel as an interactive artifact: a static gate over the bundle, a browser gate that serves the bundle with a range-capable local server and exercises every interaction headlessly, and a file-browser gate for opening from disk.
