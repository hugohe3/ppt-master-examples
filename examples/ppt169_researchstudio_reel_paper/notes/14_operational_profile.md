The takeaway is that a full four-artifact bundle takes about 89 minutes of summed stage time and about 2.6 million input and 276 thousand output tokens per paper, averaged over five papers on claude-opus-4-8, from Table 4. The bars are the per-skill subtotals: Paper2Assets 8.6 minutes, Paper2Poster 23.3, Paper2Video 28.5, Paper2Blog 16.7, Paper2Reel 12.1. The table names each skill's heaviest stage: extraction and figures for Paper2Assets at eighteen percent of input tokens; the fill loop for Paper2Poster at 14.8 minutes and eighteen percent of output; render and mux for Paper2Video; the QA gate and revision for Paper2Blog at seventeen percent of output; and the QA gate for Paper2Reel. The totals strip gives 675 turns, 2,568 thousand distinct input tokens, 108,546 thousand cached re-reads billed at roughly ten percent of fresh input, and 276 thousand output tokens. The notes below repeat the paper's qualifications: each skill runs as an isolated subprocess whose one-time context load folds into its first stage, running the three generators in parallel reduces elapsed time, and when the LaTeX source is available the pipeline can read original figure files instead of the PDF path.
