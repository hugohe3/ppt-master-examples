Still skill two. The takeaway is that the fill loop is deliberately discrete and terminates at a categorical fixed point rather than a learned-score plateau. The bar is the fullRatio axis — painted content height divided by the inner card height, read from getBoundingClientRect — quantized into the five verdicts of Section 2.2.2: EMPTY below 0.70, SPARSE from 0.70 to 0.90, FULL from 0.90 to 0.98 as the target, SPILLAGE from 0.98 to 1.10, and OVERFLOW above 1.10. The verdict alone selects the move, listed beneath: append or promote, polish up, leave untouched, polish down, drop. The Method figure is retuned under a separate figure-fill gate requiring at least ninety percent of its card on one axis. Three mechanisms damp oscillation: moves are sized by the signed pixel delta the measurement reports; the loop refuses to re-apply a move that already overshot on a section; and an on-disk round counter trips a circuit breaker that ships the best-measured state. The gate is deliberately loose at ninety percent because tightening toward ninety-eight sharply increases rounds past 0.94; a single render-time pass then stretches under-filled cards toward ninety-eight percent by growing row whitespace, without resizing figures.
