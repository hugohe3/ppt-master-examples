The takeaway is the answer to the three gaps: a native-editable dissemination workspace implemented as five skills that run in Claude Code and Codex. Figure 2 shows the pipeline — one PDF in, three editor-ready artifacts out, one shared extraction in the middle. The list on the right names the five skills in pipeline order: Paper2Assets, the shared extractor, answers G1; Paper2Poster, Paper2Video, and Paper2Blog are the editable generators whose PowerPoint and Word deliverables answer G2; Paper2Reel, the convergence layer, answers G3. The four contribution bullets are the authors' own, quoted from the end of Section 1, including the headline numbers we will see in Table 1: best among automated systems on all three aesthetic sub-criteria, best or tied-best on two of three information sub-criteria, about seventeen and a half percent above the authors' own posters on aesthetics — 3.56 against 3.03 — and overall wins on 74 and 95 of 100 papers under the two judges. The note at the bottom lists the technical ingredients: the two skill runtimes, deterministic primitives such as headless Chromium, LibreOffice with ffmpeg, and python-docx, and Edge TTS for narration; the paper states that the runtime itself is not a contribution.
