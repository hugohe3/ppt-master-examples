This is the planning surface itself, drawn to scale from the specification, with illustrative crews and bookings. Crews run down the left with their engineer counts, twelve weeks run across the top, and each bar shows committed capacity against what is still available, on one line rather than in two systems. The accreditation column is the part people underestimate: it limits who is allowed to close a job, so a crew with spare hours is not necessarily a crew that can take the next booking. The moment that earns the subscription is the flagged cell in the fourth row, where a part lead time collides with an available week. In a spreadsheet that conflict surfaces on the morning of the install. Here it surfaces before the booking is confirmed.
