Two years ago this market looked like it was failing. In twenty twenty four European heat pump sales fell twenty one per cent, the largest decline ever recorded, with Germany down almost half and France down a quarter, according to the International Energy Agency. Then twenty twenty five turned: European sales rose eleven per cent, the first growth since twenty twenty two, led by Germany at fifty five per cent in the first half, and it was the first year heat pumps outsold gas boilers in Europe. The columns show the European Union's own installation volumes, two point eight million units in twenty twenty two down to two point one one million in twenty twenty four and back up to two point three four million in twenty twenty five. What makes this durable rather than a rebound is the band along the bottom: under the recast buildings directive, member states stopped funding new stand-alone fossil boilers from the start of twenty twenty five, and national plans have to reach a complete phase-out by twenty forty. The alternative is being removed by law.
