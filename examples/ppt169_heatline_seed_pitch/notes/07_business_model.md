The pricing is a hundred and eighty pounds per engineer per month on an annual term with a five seat minimum, and these figures are illustrative. The waterfall traces one representative ten seat customer through a year: twenty one thousand six hundred pounds of gross contract value, less a launch discount, less hosting and support, less payment and partner fees, leaving fourteen thousand eight hundred and seventy pounds of gross contribution. Against a blended acquisition cost of six thousand four hundred pounds, that is a payback of five point two months, which is the number on the right and the reason expansion is the cheap growth in this business. For context on where that price sits, the published list pricing for small-trade field-service software runs from forty nine dollars a month for a single user up to four hundred and ninety nine dollars a month for five, which is a per-seat market.
