Forty four pence in every pound goes into engineering and product, and that is deliberate: the capacity planner version two and the manufacturer interface are what make the repeatable channel actually repeatable. Thirty one per cent funds go-to-market in Germany and the Netherlands, with the test being that forty per cent of new seats come from outside the United Kingdom. Thirteen per cent goes to customer success and training, held to a ten day median onboarding, which is the mechanism behind the retention number in the plan. Seven per cent funds grant and accreditation reporting automation for two national schemes, and five per cent is an explicit three month reserve rather than an implied one. Every amount, share and milestone here is illustrative.
