# ppt169_china_rail_2024_zh_sonnet

- Source: the same user-supplied materials as `ppt169_china_rail_2024_zh` — the 2024 railway statistical bulletin PDF, the World Bank route-km spreadsheet and six Wikimedia Commons CC photos with `sources/credits.tsv`. Every figure comes from the attachments; no AI image was generated
- Style: free design, generated by Claude Sonnet 5 from the same brief as the Opus deck, kept as a model comparison — pyramid mode and data-journalism style taken straight from the catalogues; five crops and tone variants derived from the six photos; five native charts, three native tables, eight preset shapes; every photo credited under CC BY / CC BY-SA with its Commons link; two Morph pairs on the bridge photo and the train badge. The final gate reports zero errors and fourteen pages with compact-authoring advisories, left as generated
- Language: Chinese throughout; faces are SimSun for titles, Microsoft YaHei for body and Consolas for figures, `lang` is zh-CN
- Canvas is determined during authoring and recorded in spec_lock.md (Default) or the first SVG (Quick).
- Created: 20260912
- Regenerate: `python3 skills/ppt-master/scripts/svg_quality_checker.py <this dir> --canonical-authoring --stage final --json`, then `python3 skills/ppt-master/scripts/svg_to_pptx.py <this dir>` (the sidecar `animations.json` carries two Morph pairs and the entrances); add `--native-charts-and-tables` for the five editable charts and three tables

## Directories

- `svg_output/`: raw SVG output
- `svg_final/`: self-contained SVG visual preview; may be inserted manually as an SVG image, but PowerPoint Convert to Shape is unsupported
- `images/`: runtime image pool (CC photos and their derivatives, compressed to JPEG)
- `icons/`: project icon set (tabler-outline)
- `notes/`: speaker notes
- `sources/`: the bulletin PDF and its Markdown, the World Bank spreadsheet and its Markdown, the six photos and `credits.tsv`
- `analysis/`: image_analysis.csv
- `exports/`: final native DrawingML pptx deliverables (standard and `_native_charts_tables`)
