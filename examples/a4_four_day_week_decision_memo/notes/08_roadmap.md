The roadmap exists to make the decision reversible, and to fix the points of reversal before anyone becomes attached to the policy. Phase zero runs ten weeks under the chief operating officer and does the unglamorous work: cutting meeting length and frequency, reforming email etiquette, agreeing the metric set, and agreeing what we will tell clients — Portugal used eight preparation sessions over three months on exactly those questions. Phase one operates for six months under the practice heads. Gate A at month three asks whether the kill criteria are holding, and it should be read as the high-water mark rather than the trend, because most Portuguese indicators slipped between three and six months for reasons the authors could not fully explain. Phase two evaluates in month seven against the non-participating practice groups as a control — the design Portugal had and the UK 2022 pilot lacked. Gate B decides.
