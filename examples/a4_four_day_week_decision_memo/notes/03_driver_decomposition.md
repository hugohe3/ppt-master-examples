Recoverable hours came, in every pilot, from shorter and fewer meetings, reformed email etiquette, protected focus periods and automated reporting; the German participants named meeting culture and reduced distraction as their main levers. Redesign effort is the strongest predictor in the whole evidence base: only 8 percent of Portuguese companies that made two or more organisational changes reverted to five days, against 38 percent of those that made none or one. Retention economics look attractive but must be handled carefully — resignations fell from 2.0 to 0.8 per hundred in the UK pilot, and the most-cited UK replacement cost of thirty thousand six hundred and fourteen pounds is a 2014 figure that is not inflation-adjusted, so any saving derived from it is arithmetic, not a finding.
