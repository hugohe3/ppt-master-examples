# song_diancha_xhs

- Canvas format: xiaohongshu (1242×1660)
- Created: 20260829
- Runtime: Quick Generate, free design (topic-only input → topic-research → AI watercolor illustrations)

## Directories

- `svg_output/`: authored SVG cards (native-export source)
- `svg_final/`: self-contained preview SVGs (images embedded; rendered by the gallery viewer)
- `images/`: AI-generated hero images and transparent slices (compressed for distribution) plus the generation manifest
- `analysis/`: image facts regenerated by `analyze_images.py`
- `notes/`: speaker notes
- `sources/`: research supplement and fact provenance produced by the topic-research stage
- `animations.json`: transition and per-object entrance choreography
- `exports/`: exported PPTX (with speaker notes and animations)

## Regenerate the PPTX

```bash
python3 skills/ppt-master/scripts/svg_quality_checker.py <this_dir> --quick-generate --canonical-authoring --stage final --json
python3 skills/ppt-master/scripts/svg_to_pptx.py <this_dir> --quick-generate --with-notes
```
