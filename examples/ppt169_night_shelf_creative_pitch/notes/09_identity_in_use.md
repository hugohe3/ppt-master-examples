This is the system underneath, and it is small enough that you can keep running it yourself after we have gone. One type voice: a bookish serif for everything the campaign says, and a quiet sans for everything the shop adds later, so your own notices never look like they are shouting. Four colours, each doing exactly one job. Night ink is always the ground. Lamplight is the light in the picture and the mark — it is never used for type, which is the rule that keeps everything legible. Paper cream is anything a reader physically touches. Fern is only ever your own signature, so a customer learns to read it as the shop speaking rather than the campaign. And the crescent on the shelf rule works from a poster down to a bookmark without redrawing it. The one prohibition worth remembering: red only ever appears when we are talking about a screen.
