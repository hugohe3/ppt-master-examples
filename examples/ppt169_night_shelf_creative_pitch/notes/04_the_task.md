Which gives the work a very specific job. Make Fog and Fern the shop a reader thinks of at nine in the evening. Not the shop they visit on a Saturday afternoon — the one that owns the last hour of their day. And it has to happen inside three constraints, all of which are illustrative for this example client but realistic for a shop your size. No paid media at all beyond the frontage and the channels you already own. One season, six weeks from the first paste-up to the last event. And five people, every one of whom is already working a full shift.
