Here is the truth the work is built on, and it is a truth about time, not about taste. The Bureau of Labor Statistics measures how Americans actually spend a day, and in the 2025 averages, reading for personal interest comes to about sixteen minutes. Television comes to two hours and thirty-seven. That is the same day. Look at how the gap changes with age. Among fifteen to twenty-four year olds it is ten minutes of reading against one hour forty-six of television. Among people sixty-five and over, reading more than triples, but television nearly doubles as well. Nobody in this room has to argue that people should love books more. They already do. What they do not have is room.
