卖出去只是开始。公开披露的计划是,自 2026 年 3 月到 2027 年 3 月底在海外建成 6000 座闪充站,其中欧洲 3000 座、美洲 2000 座、亚太 1000 座,这是比亚迪集团品牌及公关处总经理李云飞 2026 年 7 月给出的数字。量级很清楚,但推进通常慢于计划,原因也很具体:充电难和维修难要同时靠售后服务中心、零件仓库和充换电设施解决,基础设施投入大、回报周期长,再加上用地审批和跨行业协作,进度往往不及预期。对合作伙伴来说,这一段恰恰是可以共同出力的地方。

Selling the car is only the start. The disclosed plan is six thousand overseas fast-charging sites between March 2026 and the end of March 2027 — three thousand in Europe, two thousand in the Americas, one thousand in Asia-Pacific — a figure given by BYD's brand and public relations head Li Yunfei in July 2026. The scale is clear, but build-out usually runs behind plan for concrete reasons: charging and repair gaps have to be closed together through service centres, parts warehouses and charging sites; the capital outlay is heavy and payback slow; and land permits and cross-industry coordination add delay. For partners, this is exactly where joint effort pays.
