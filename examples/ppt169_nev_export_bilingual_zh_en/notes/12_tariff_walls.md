第四部分讲规则。两道墙的性质完全不同。欧盟自 2024 年 10 月 31 日起征收确定性反补贴税,按企业分级:特斯拉 7.8%,比亚迪 17%,吉利 18.8%,配合调查但未被抽样的企业加权平均 20.3%,上汽 35.3%。关键是这笔税叠加在原有 10% 的汽车进口关税之上,所以综合税负最高可达 45.3%。美国是另一种做法:301 条款下,中国产电动汽车关税自 2024 年 9 月 27 日起统一提高到 100%,同一批还包括光伏电池 50%、动力电池与关键矿产 25%,发布机构是美国贸易代表办公室。分级税率意味着不同企业的欧洲策略会不一样,一刀切则基本关闭了整车路径。

Part four is about rules, and the two walls are different in kind. The EU has charged definitive countervailing duties since 31 October 2024, tiered by producer: Tesla 7.8 percent, BYD 17, Geely 18.8, a 20.3 percent weighted average for cooperating but unsampled producers, and SAIC 35.3. Crucially these sit on top of the existing 10 percent car import duty, so the combined burden reaches 45.3 percent. The United States took the other route: under Section 301 the tariff on Chinese EVs rose to a flat 100 percent on 27 September 2024, in a package that also raised solar cells to 50 percent and EV batteries and key minerals to 25, announced by the US Trade Representative. Tiered rates mean each company's European strategy differs; a flat rate closes the shipment route almost entirely.
