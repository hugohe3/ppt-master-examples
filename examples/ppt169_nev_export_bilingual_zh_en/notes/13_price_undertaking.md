2026 年出现了一个新变量。当年 1 月,欧盟发布《关于提交价格承诺申请的指导文件》,中国商务部同日通报。机制本身是最低进口价格,企业承诺不低于某个价格销售,以此替代 17% 到 35.3% 的反补贴税,也就是替代那个最高 45.3% 的综合税负。价格怎么定,依据是调查期到岸价加上反补贴税额,并对标欧盟同类非补贴纯电车的售价。还有一个附加条件:企业需要承诺在欧洲建厂的投资规模与时间表,这一点正好和第三部分的本地化判断对上。必须说清楚的是,报道只说框架已经达成、进入软着陆阶段,没有明确生效日期,所以本页按未生效处理,这也是我们建议双方共同跟踪的第一件事。

A new variable appeared in 2026. In January the EU issued its guidance on submitting price-undertaking applications and China's Ministry of Commerce notified it the same day. The mechanism is a minimum import price: a company commits to selling at or above a set price instead of paying the 17 to 35.3 percent duties, that is, instead of the combined burden of up to 45.3 percent. The price is derived from the CIF value in the investigation period plus the duty amount, benchmarked against comparable non-subsidised EU electric cars. One condition is attached: the company must commit to the scale and timetable of a European plant, which lines up exactly with the localization argument in part three. To be precise, reporting says only that a framework has been agreed and the case has entered a soft landing; no effective date has been announced, so we treat it as not yet in force, and it is the first thing we suggest both sides track together.
