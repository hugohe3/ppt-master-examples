第三部分是本简报最想传达的判断。建厂常被理解为规模扩张,但从这几年的公开动作看,它同时回答三个问题。第一步出口整车,受关税和运输成本直接约束;第二步在当地生产,在欧洲是直接对冲关税并满足当地对投资的要求,在东南亚和拉美更多是服务本地与区域市场;第三步是当地网络,充电和售后决定复购,也决定品牌能不能留下来。这个三步框架是我们按调研事实整理的读法,不是某一家机构的原话。结论是,2026 年的增量更多来自海外产能,而不是整车出口。

Part three carries the judgement this briefing wants to leave you with. Building plants is usually read as expansion, but the public record of the past few years shows it answering three questions at once. Shipping finished cars is directly exposed to tariffs and freight. Producing locally hedges tariffs and meets investment expectations in Europe, while in Southeast Asia and Latin America it mainly serves local and regional demand. Local networks come third: charging and after-sales decide repeat purchase, and therefore whether a brand stays. This three-step framework is our reading of the sourced facts, not a quotation. The conclusion is that the 2026 increment comes more from overseas capacity than from shipments.
