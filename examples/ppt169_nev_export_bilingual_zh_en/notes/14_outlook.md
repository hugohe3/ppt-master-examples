对 2026 年,两个公开预测都指向个位数增长。中国汽车工业协会 2026 年 1 月 14 日预测全年汽车出口 740 万辆,同比增长 4.3%;中国电动汽车百人会理事长张永伟给出的判断是冲击 800 万辆。对比 2025 年实际的 21.1%,总量增速明显回落。更值得注意的是更长的判断:车百会预计到 2030 年,中国汽车含出口在内的海外产销达到 1000 万辆,其中海外生产销售的占比有望冲击 50%。如果这个方向成立,今天讨论的重点就不该是出口量,而是海外产能。

Both public forecasts for 2026 point to single-digit growth. On 14 January 2026 CAAM projected 7.4 million vehicle exports, up 4.3 percent, while Zhang Yongwei of the China EV100 put the figure nearer eight million. Against the 21.1 percent actually recorded in 2025, volume growth clearly slows. The longer view matters more: the EV100 expects overseas production and sales, exports included, to reach ten million units by 2030, with locally produced and sold vehicles possibly approaching half of that. If that direction holds, the subject of conversations like this one should be overseas capacity rather than export volume.
