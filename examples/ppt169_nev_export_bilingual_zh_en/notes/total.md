# 01_cover

这份简报只讲一件事:2025 年中国新能源汽车出口翻了一倍,但真正值得两边同事一起讨论的,是增长方式已经变了。全部数据来自公开发布来源,以中国汽车工业协会口径为主,乘联分会口径在同一页并列标注,每页底部都写明机构与年份,方便会后各自引用核对。今天大约三十分钟,四个部分。

This briefing makes one point: China's NEV exports doubled in 2025, but what matters for both sides is that the growth model itself has changed. Every figure comes from public sources, CAAM first, with CPCA shown alongside on the same page, and each page footer names the institution and the year so you can cite it afterwards. About thirty minutes, four parts.

---

# 02_agenda

四个部分依次回答四个问题:第一部分先把规模和口径说清楚,出口了多少、按谁的口径;第二部分讲卖到哪里、谁在卖;第三部分解释为什么开始在当地造车;第四部分是规则怎么变、2026 年怎么看。部分顺序就是论证顺序,如果只关心某一段,可以直接跳到那一部分。

The four parts answer four questions in order: how much was exported and on whose count; where the cars went and who shipped them; why plants are replacing shipments; and how the rules are changing into 2026. The order of the parts is the order of the argument, so feel free to jump to the part you care about.

---

# 03_calibres

在看任何数字之前,先讲口径,否则两边引用的数据永远对不上。中国汽车工业协会的口径是 2025 年新能源汽车出口 261.5 万辆,这是本简报的主口径;乘联分会同一年给出的是 343 万辆,统计范围更宽。两个数字都对,差别在统计范围,所以我们既不相加也不折中,而是在需要时并列标注。本页的口径对照是编者按调研来源整理的,不是任何一家机构的原文表述。

Before any number, a word on calibres, otherwise the two sides will never quote the same figure. CAAM puts 2025 NEV exports at 2.615 million units, and that is the primary calibre here. CPCA gives 3.43 million for the same year on a wider definition. Both are correct; the difference is scope, so we never add them together or average them. This comparison is our own reading of the sources, not a quotation from either institution.

# 04_volumes

2025 年中国汽车出口总量 709.8 万辆,同比增长 21.1%。拆开看,新能源汽车 261.5 万辆,同比增长 103.7%,几乎正好翻了一倍;传统燃料汽车 448.3 万辆,同比下降 2%。也就是说,这一年的增量全部来自新能源,燃油车已经在收缩。按这两个数计算,新能源约占出口总量的 36.8%,这个百分比是我们自己算的,不是来源里的原始数字。

Total vehicle exports reached 7.098 million units in 2025, up 21.1 percent. Within that, NEVs were 2.615 million, up 103.7 percent — almost exactly double — while conventional vehicles fell 2 percent to 4.483 million. All of the growth came from NEVs; the combustion side is already shrinking. From those two figures NEVs are about 36.8 percent of exports, and that share is our calculation rather than a number taken from the source.

---

# 05_four_year_step

把四年放在一起看,增长不是匀速的,而是两级台阶。2022 年 67.9 万辆,2023 年 120.3 万辆,到 2024 年只有 128.4 万辆,几乎走平,增幅大约 6.7%;2025 年直接跳到 261.5 万辆。所以用平均增速去推 2026 年是危险的,这条曲线的形状本身就说明它受政策和产能节奏影响很大。

Seen across four years the growth is not steady, it comes in two steps. From 679,000 units in 2022 to 1.203 million in 2023, then almost flat at 1.284 million in 2024, a rise of only about 7 percent, and then a jump to 2.615 million in 2025. Extrapolating from an average growth rate would be misleading; the shape of this line shows how strongly policy and capacity timing drive it.

---

# 06_two_calibres

这一页把两个口径放在一起。中汽协 261.5 万辆,统计的是新能源整车出口;乘联分会 343 万辆,范围更宽,增速口径也不同,是同比增长 70%。两者不是矛盾,是范围不同,所以引用时一定要带机构名。还有一个值得注意的方向性事实:同期整车出口均价从 2024 年的 1.8 万美元降到 1.6 万美元,量在增、单价在降,这对海外经销商的定价和库存策略是直接相关的。

This page puts the two calibres side by side. CAAM counts 2.615 million NEVs; CPCA counts 3.43 million on a wider definition, with growth of 70 percent on its own basis. They do not contradict each other, they measure different things, so always cite the institution. One more directional fact matters: the average export price fell from eighteen thousand US dollars in 2024 to sixteen thousand in 2025. More units at a lower unit price bears directly on pricing and inventory decisions overseas.

---

# 07_destinations

出口地图在 2025 年发生了位置变化。墨西哥以 62.52 万辆首次超过俄罗斯,成为中国汽车出口第一大目的国;俄罗斯 58.27 万辆,阿联酋 57.2 万辆。前三名之间的差距不到 6 万辆,谁排第一并不稳定,更重要的是三个市场分属北美、独联体和中东三个完全不同的准入环境。第四到第六位是英国、巴西和沙特,本简报没有拿到可核对的确切数值,所以没有画进图里。提醒一下,这一页是乘联分会的整车出口统计,包含燃油车。

The export map shifted in 2025. Mexico took first place for the first time with 625,200 units, ahead of Russia at 582,700 and the UAE at 572,000. The top three are within sixty thousand units of each other, so the ranking is not stable; what matters more is that the three sit in three completely different access regimes — North America, the CIS and the Middle East. Fourth to sixth are the UK, Brazil and Saudi Arabia, but we have no verified figures for them, so they are not charted. Note this page counts all vehicles, combustion included.

# 08_exporters

出口量第一和增速第一不是同一家,这一点对合作方判断对手很关键。奇瑞以 134.4 万辆继续保持中国车企出口第一,同比增长 17.4%,集团总销量 280.6 万辆;上汽出口 107.1 万辆;比亚迪海外销量 104.96 万辆,同比增长 145%,首次突破百万辆。长安 63.7 万辆,长城 50.6 万辆,增速都在两位数以内。补充三个事实:上汽 MG 品牌在欧洲卖了 30 万辆以上,同比接近 30%;比亚迪的出口约占它自己总销量的 23%;上汽通用五菱出口 26.7 万辆,同比增长 128.6%。

The largest exporter and the fastest-growing one are not the same company, which matters when you assess a counterpart. Chery remains first at 1.344 million units, up 17.4 percent, on group sales of 2.806 million. SAIC exported 1.071 million. BYD sold 1.0496 million abroad, up 145 percent, passing one million for the first time. Changan and Great Wall grew in the teens, at 637,000 and 506,000. Three additions: MG sold over 300,000 in Europe, up nearly 30 percent; exports are about 23 percent of BYD's own sales; and SGMW exported 267,000, up 128.6 percent.

# 09_localization

第三部分是本简报最想传达的判断。建厂常被理解为规模扩张,但从这几年的公开动作看,它同时回答三个问题。第一步出口整车,受关税和运输成本直接约束;第二步在当地生产,在欧洲是直接对冲关税并满足当地对投资的要求,在东南亚和拉美更多是服务本地与区域市场;第三步是当地网络,充电和售后决定复购,也决定品牌能不能留下来。这个三步框架是我们按调研事实整理的读法,不是某一家机构的原话。结论是,2026 年的增量更多来自海外产能,而不是整车出口。

Part three carries the judgement this briefing wants to leave you with. Building plants is usually read as expansion, but the public record of the past few years shows it answering three questions at once. Shipping finished cars is directly exposed to tariffs and freight. Producing locally hedges tariffs and meets investment expectations in Europe, while in Southeast Asia and Latin America it mainly serves local and regional demand. Local networks come third: charging and after-sales decide repeat purchase, and therefore whether a brand stays. This three-step framework is our reading of the sourced facts, not a quotation. The conclusion is that the 2026 increment comes more from overseas capacity than from shipments.

# 10_overseas_plants

四个已经落地的基地说明了这两种目的。泰国罗勇府工厂 2024 年 7 月 4 日竣工投产,年产能约 15 万辆,同一天下线了比亚迪第 800 万辆新能源汽车。巴西卡马萨里基地 2025 年 7 月 1 日首车下线,规划产能 15 万辆,投资 55 亿雷亚尔,约合 71 亿元人民币,预计带来 2 万个就业岗位。匈牙利塞格德工厂投资 40 亿欧元,约合 350 亿元人民币,最大年产能 30 万辆,初期约 15 万辆,2026 年第一季度试生产、第二季度量产。西班牙巴塞罗那自贸区的合资工厂 2024 年 11 月 23 日投产首款车型,有望带来 1250 个当地岗位。泰国和巴西偏向服务本地市场,匈牙利和西班牙则明显是对冲关税。

Four bases already on the ground show both purposes. Rayong in Thailand came on stream on 4 July 2024 with capacity of about 150,000 units a year, the same day BYD's eight-millionth NEV came off the line. Camaçari in Brazil produced its first car on 1 July 2025, planned at 150,000 units, on an investment of 5.5 billion reais, roughly 7.1 billion yuan, with an expected twenty thousand jobs. Szeged in Hungary carries a 4 billion euro investment, about 35 billion yuan, a peak capacity of 300,000 units and about 150,000 initially, with trial production in the first quarter of 2026 and volume production in the second. The joint-venture plant in the Barcelona free zone started its first model on 23 November 2024 and is expected to bring 1,250 local jobs. Thailand and Brazil lean towards serving local demand; Hungary and Spain are clearly about tariffs.

# 11_charging_network

卖出去只是开始。公开披露的计划是,自 2026 年 3 月到 2027 年 3 月底在海外建成 6000 座闪充站,其中欧洲 3000 座、美洲 2000 座、亚太 1000 座,这是比亚迪集团品牌及公关处总经理李云飞 2026 年 7 月给出的数字。量级很清楚,但推进通常慢于计划,原因也很具体:充电难和维修难要同时靠售后服务中心、零件仓库和充换电设施解决,基础设施投入大、回报周期长,再加上用地审批和跨行业协作,进度往往不及预期。对合作伙伴来说,这一段恰恰是可以共同出力的地方。

Selling the car is only the start. The disclosed plan is six thousand overseas fast-charging sites between March 2026 and the end of March 2027 — three thousand in Europe, two thousand in the Americas, one thousand in Asia-Pacific — a figure given by BYD's brand and public relations head Li Yunfei in July 2026. The scale is clear, but build-out usually runs behind plan for concrete reasons: charging and repair gaps have to be closed together through service centres, parts warehouses and charging sites; the capital outlay is heavy and payback slow; and land permits and cross-industry coordination add delay. For partners, this is exactly where joint effort pays.

# 12_tariff_walls

第四部分讲规则。两道墙的性质完全不同。欧盟自 2024 年 10 月 31 日起征收确定性反补贴税,按企业分级:特斯拉 7.8%,比亚迪 17%,吉利 18.8%,配合调查但未被抽样的企业加权平均 20.3%,上汽 35.3%。关键是这笔税叠加在原有 10% 的汽车进口关税之上,所以综合税负最高可达 45.3%。美国是另一种做法:301 条款下,中国产电动汽车关税自 2024 年 9 月 27 日起统一提高到 100%,同一批还包括光伏电池 50%、动力电池与关键矿产 25%,发布机构是美国贸易代表办公室。分级税率意味着不同企业的欧洲策略会不一样,一刀切则基本关闭了整车路径。

Part four is about rules, and the two walls are different in kind. The EU has charged definitive countervailing duties since 31 October 2024, tiered by producer: Tesla 7.8 percent, BYD 17, Geely 18.8, a 20.3 percent weighted average for cooperating but unsampled producers, and SAIC 35.3. Crucially these sit on top of the existing 10 percent car import duty, so the combined burden reaches 45.3 percent. The United States took the other route: under Section 301 the tariff on Chinese EVs rose to a flat 100 percent on 27 September 2024, in a package that also raised solar cells to 50 percent and EV batteries and key minerals to 25, announced by the US Trade Representative. Tiered rates mean each company's European strategy differs; a flat rate closes the shipment route almost entirely.

---

# 13_price_undertaking

2026 年出现了一个新变量。当年 1 月,欧盟发布《关于提交价格承诺申请的指导文件》,中国商务部同日通报。机制本身是最低进口价格,企业承诺不低于某个价格销售,以此替代 17% 到 35.3% 的反补贴税,也就是替代那个最高 45.3% 的综合税负。价格怎么定,依据是调查期到岸价加上反补贴税额,并对标欧盟同类非补贴纯电车的售价。还有一个附加条件:企业需要承诺在欧洲建厂的投资规模与时间表,这一点正好和第三部分的本地化判断对上。必须说清楚的是,报道只说框架已经达成、进入软着陆阶段,没有明确生效日期,所以本页按未生效处理,这也是我们建议双方共同跟踪的第一件事。

A new variable appeared in 2026. In January the EU issued its guidance on submitting price-undertaking applications and China's Ministry of Commerce notified it the same day. The mechanism is a minimum import price: a company commits to selling at or above a set price instead of paying the 17 to 35.3 percent duties, that is, instead of the combined burden of up to 45.3 percent. The price is derived from the CIF value in the investigation period plus the duty amount, benchmarked against comparable non-subsidised EU electric cars. One condition is attached: the company must commit to the scale and timetable of a European plant, which lines up exactly with the localization argument in part three. To be precise, reporting says only that a framework has been agreed and the case has entered a soft landing; no effective date has been announced, so we treat it as not yet in force, and it is the first thing we suggest both sides track together.

# 14_outlook

对 2026 年,两个公开预测都指向个位数增长。中国汽车工业协会 2026 年 1 月 14 日预测全年汽车出口 740 万辆,同比增长 4.3%;中国电动汽车百人会理事长张永伟给出的判断是冲击 800 万辆。对比 2025 年实际的 21.1%,总量增速明显回落。更值得注意的是更长的判断:车百会预计到 2030 年,中国汽车含出口在内的海外产销达到 1000 万辆,其中海外生产销售的占比有望冲击 50%。如果这个方向成立,今天讨论的重点就不该是出口量,而是海外产能。

Both public forecasts for 2026 point to single-digit growth. On 14 January 2026 CAAM projected 7.4 million vehicle exports, up 4.3 percent, while Zhang Yongwei of the China EV100 put the figure nearer eight million. Against the 21.1 percent actually recorded in 2025, volume growth clearly slows. The longer view matters more: the EV100 expects overseas production and sales, exports included, to reach ten million units by 2030, with locally produced and sold vehicles possibly approaching half of that. If that direction holds, the subject of conversations like this one should be overseas capacity rather than export volume.

---

# 15_takeaway

收一下。2025 年出口确实翻倍到 261.5 万辆,中汽协口径;但下一阶段比的不是出口量,而是本地产能、网络与规则适配。给双方三件可以马上做的事:第一,口径对齐,以后引用任何数字都标注机构和年份,避免会议上出现两套数据;第二,本地化配套,按各基地的时间表去对齐产能、零部件与售后计划,尤其是匈牙利 2026 年第二季度量产这个节点;第三,规则跟踪,共同盯住最低进口价格机制的生效进展和各市场准入变化。全部数据的口径与年份都在各页页脚,会后可以直接引用。

To close. Exports did double, to 2.615 million units on the CAAM calibre, but the next stage is about local capacity, networks and rule alignment rather than volume. Three things both sides can act on now. First, align the numbers: cite the institution and the year for every figure so we never argue from two datasets. Second, match the plants: align capacity, parts and after-sales plans to each base's timetable, especially volume production at Szeged in the second quarter of 2026. Third, track the rules: watch the minimum-import-price timetable and market-access changes together. Calibres and years are in every page footer, ready to cite after the meeting.
