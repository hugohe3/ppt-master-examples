四个已经落地的基地说明了这两种目的。泰国罗勇府工厂 2024 年 7 月 4 日竣工投产,年产能约 15 万辆,同一天下线了比亚迪第 800 万辆新能源汽车。巴西卡马萨里基地 2025 年 7 月 1 日首车下线,规划产能 15 万辆,投资 55 亿雷亚尔,约合 71 亿元人民币,预计带来 2 万个就业岗位。匈牙利塞格德工厂投资 40 亿欧元,约合 350 亿元人民币,最大年产能 30 万辆,初期约 15 万辆,2026 年第一季度试生产、第二季度量产。西班牙巴塞罗那自贸区的合资工厂 2024 年 11 月 23 日投产首款车型,有望带来 1250 个当地岗位。泰国和巴西偏向服务本地市场,匈牙利和西班牙则明显是对冲关税。

Four bases already on the ground show both purposes. Rayong in Thailand came on stream on 4 July 2024 with capacity of about 150,000 units a year, the same day BYD's eight-millionth NEV came off the line. Camaçari in Brazil produced its first car on 1 July 2025, planned at 150,000 units, on an investment of 5.5 billion reais, roughly 7.1 billion yuan, with an expected twenty thousand jobs. Szeged in Hungary carries a 4 billion euro investment, about 35 billion yuan, a peak capacity of 300,000 units and about 150,000 initially, with trial production in the first quarter of 2026 and volume production in the second. The joint-venture plant in the Barcelona free zone started its first model on 23 November 2024 and is expected to bring 1,250 local jobs. Thailand and Brazil lean towards serving local demand; Hungary and Spain are clearly about tariffs.
