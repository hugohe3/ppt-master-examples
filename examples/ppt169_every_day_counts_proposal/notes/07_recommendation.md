Here is what we are proposing. Build one attendance system with three tiers and staff it inside the district. Tier one is universal prevention for every student. Tier two is early intervention for the students who are slipping toward the ten percent line. Tier three is coordinated case support for the students already in crisis. This is not our invention; the Framework for Action identifies a comprehensive multi tiered system of supports, combining prevention with early and consistent interventions, as the most effective response. The division of labour we are proposing is that Lantern designs the system and trains the site leads, and Riverbend runs it. The staffing behind that, one attendance lead per site and one district coordinator, is an illustrative figure at this stage.
