Proposals are usually vague at exactly the point where they should be precise, so this page states the boundary. Nine deliverables, each marked included in a given year, optional at additional cost, or not included, with the status written as a word in the cell so it survives being printed or forwarded in black and white. The home visit protocol and the community partner agreements are optional in year one and included in year two. The student information system integration build is not included at all, because that is a district information technology procurement and pricing it here would be pretending to a control we do not have. Four things only Riverbend can supply are listed beside the grid: data access within two weeks, a named executive sponsor, released time for site leads, and a family contact consent policy. The scope shown is illustrative and would be fixed in a statement of work.
